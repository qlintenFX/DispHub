# Privacy policy

DispHub runs entirely on your PC. It has no account system and collects no telemetry or usage data.

**What it stores.** Your profiles and settings, a record of your displays' original colours (so they can be
restored), simple usage counters (first start date and how often profiles switched, used for the 7-day Pro trial and
to decide when to ask for a Store rating) and log files. These stay on your PC in `%LocalAppData%\DispHub`, or in
the app's private folder when installed from the Microsoft Store. Uninstalling the Store version removes them, except
the first start date, which the Store version also keeps in Windows Credential Manager (as DispHub/FirstRun) so that
reinstalling doesn't start a new trial. You can delete it there.

**What it reads.** To switch profiles per app, DispHub checks which program is in the foreground. This is used
immediately and never sent anywhere; when it's a program from one of your app rules, the rule remembers where that
program is, to show its icon. If you click Use my location on the Schedule page, Windows' location
service gives DispHub your approximate position to work out sunrise and sunset; it's saved in your settings as
rounded coordinates and never sent anywhere by DispHub.

**Network.** DispHub doesn't connect to the internet and doesn't send your data anywhere. Purchases and ratings in
the Microsoft Store version, and Windows' location service, are handled by Microsoft under the
[Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

**Contact.** Questions about this policy: open an issue at <https://github.com/qlintenFX/DispHub/issues>.
