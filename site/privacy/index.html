Privacy policy.

DispHub runs entirely on your PC. It has no account system and collects no telemetry or usage data.

What it stores

Your profiles and settings, a record of your displays' original colours (so they can be restored), simple usage counters (first start date and number of profile switches, used to decide when to ask for a Store rating) and log files. These stay on your PC in %LocalAppData%\DispHub, or in the app's private folder when installed from the Microsoft Store. Uninstalling the Store version removes them.

What it reads

To switch profiles per app, DispHub checks which program is in the foreground. This is used immediately and never stored or sent anywhere. If you click Use my location on the Schedule page, Windows' location service gives DispHub your approximate position to work out sunrise and sunset; it's saved in your settings as rounded coordinates and never sent anywhere by DispHub.

Network

DispHub doesn't connect to the internet and doesn't send your data anywhere. Purchases and ratings in the Microsoft Store version, and Windows' location service, are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits to keep the service secure; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The Microsoft Store button loads its image from Microsoft.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/qlintenFX/DispHub/issues.
