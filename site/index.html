Questions.

Do people watching my stream see the changed colours?

No. DispHub changes colours in your graphics card's output, after Windows has drawn the screen, so OBS, Discord, Teams and screenshots capture the original colours. Your viewers see a normal picture while you see your profile.

Will it slow down my games?

No. DispHub changes colours in your graphics card's output, so games draw exactly as before. DispHub itself waits in the notification area and uses very little memory.

Which graphics cards support vibrance?

NVIDIA and Intel, including the built-in screen of laptops with Intel graphics. AMD support is in beta, so please tell us how it works for you. Brightness, contrast, gamma and colour temperature work on any graphics card.

My night profile isn't as warm as I set it.

Windows limits how far colours can change by default. Turn on Settings > Displays > Full colour range in DispHub. It asks for administrator approval once.

Does it work with HDR?

Vibrance does. Windows doesn't allow gamma changes while HDR is on, so brightness, contrast, gamma and colour temperature pause until you turn HDR off.

What if DispHub crashes?

DispHub saves your original colours before it changes anything and puts them back when you pause or quit, even after a crash. Your screen is never left with a profile you can't undo.

What does Pro cost, and is it a subscription?

Pro is a single purchase in the app through the Microsoft Store, in your local currency. There's no subscription and no ads, and the free version has no time limit.

Is DispHub open source?

Yes. DispHub is free software under the GNU General Public License v3.0 or later, and the code is on GitHub.

What data does it collect?

None. There's no account and no telemetry, and DispHub doesn't connect to the internet. Your profiles and settings stay on your PC. Read the privacy policy.
